# CERTIFICATES.md - certificate and script provenance

CAS systems: Magma V2.29-7 (A9, Linux) and Sage 10.8 (macOS). Every load-bearing CAS fact
is two-system checked; every polynomial cofactor consumed by a `linear_combination` in the
Lean source is reproduced **verbatim** in that source and checked by the kernel - the
kernel check of the verbatim cofactor, not the CAS transcript, is the standing guarantee.

## Consumers -> artifacts

- **Forward direction certificates** (`curve_cleared`, `aden_cleared`, `adP6_cover`,
  `forward_core` cofactors; reconstruction data `wN`, `wD`, `K1`, sign covers):
  generated by `scripts/sage/wp3_stage2.sage` / `wp3_stage3.sage`, raw store
  `scripts/wp3_stage3_certs.txt`; inversion cross-checked by `scripts/magma/wp3_invert.m`
  and `scripts/sage/wp3_invert_verify.sage`.
- **Cleared gate identities** (`gate1_cleared`, `gate2_cleared`): cofactors are verbatim
  in `Thm7Prime/Master.lean` (Thm7RepairGates lineage, md5 in the in-file assembly note)
  and kernel-checked; the original elimination transcript is not retained - the identity
  is reproducible from the stated gate relations in any Groebner engine, and the kernel
  check is the guarantee of record.
- **Disclosed axiom** `rank_E576i2`: `scripts/magma/wp1_rank576i2.m` (unconditional
  2-descent, RankBounds), Sage cross-check.
- **Statement-fidelity gate** (design-time CAS leg): `scripts/sage/wp1_fidelity.sage`;
  kernel counterpart now in `Thm7Prime/Fidelity.lean`.
- **[MC] cross-checks**: `scripts/sage/wp4_mc.sage`; rank corrections and Stroeker audit
  under `scripts/rank/`.
