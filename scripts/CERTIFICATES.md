# CERTIFICATES.md - certificate and script provenance

CAS systems: Magma V2.29-7 (A9, Linux) and Sage 10.8 (macOS).  "Two-system checked"
below means both systems proved the same rigorous statement; entries whose Status
column says otherwise (analytic evidence, partial runs) are so labelled and are NOT
covered by that phrase.  Every polynomial cofactor consumed by a `linear_combination`
in the Lean source is reproduced **verbatim** in that source and checked by the
kernel - the kernel check, not the CAS transcript, is the standing guarantee.
Hashes: SHA-256 is authoritative; md5 retained for continuity with earlier records.

## Consumers -> artifacts

- **Forward direction certificates** (`curve_cleared`, `aden_cleared`, `adP6_cover`,
  `forward_core` cofactors; reconstruction data `wN`, `wD`, `K1`, sign covers):
  generated by `scripts/sage/wp3_stage2.sage` / `wp3_stage3.sage`, raw store
  `scripts/wp3_stage3_certs.txt`; inversion cross-checked by `scripts/magma/wp3_invert.m`
  and `scripts/sage/wp3_invert_verify.sage`.  Two-system, rigorous.
- **Cleared gate identities** (`gate1_cleared`, `gate2_cleared`): cofactors verbatim
  in `Thm7Prime/Master.lean` and kernel-checked; the original elimination transcript
  is not retained - the identity is reproducible from the stated gate relations in
  any Groebner engine, and the kernel check is the guarantee of record.
- **Disclosed axiom** `rank_E576i2`: `scripts/magma/wp1_rank576i2.m` (unconditional
  2-descent, RankBounds), Sage cross-check.  Two-system, rigorous.  Off the main
  line (historical route only).
- **Statement-fidelity gate**: `scripts/sage/wp1_fidelity.sage`; kernel counterpart
  in `Thm7Prime/Fidelity.lean`.
- **[MC] cross-checks**: `scripts/sage/wp4_mc.sage`.
- **Rank audit of BM Tables 4/5** (`scripts/rank/`): single-system Sage audit of the
  published tables; per-file Status below.  These logs are NOT load-bearing for any
  theorem in this repository (the main line is rank-free); they document the audit
  of the source paper's tables.
